minor — The Batch encoder reserves a scratch buffer with capacity equal to all remaining payload room (up to 16 MiB) before it knows the actual encoded size. Small or empty batches therefore incur large allocations; nesting can keep one such oversized buffer per active level. Size the batch first or grow scratch storage as children are appended, while preserving the pre-copy budget check.
minor — The `len(records) > math.MaxUint32` check is listed after the child walk and scratch construction. On 64-bit systems an oversized Batch can therefore traverse and encode all children before being rejected, despite the plan's stated intent to reject an unrepresentable count before walking children. Move the count validation to the start of the Batch case.
No blocker or major issue remains.
