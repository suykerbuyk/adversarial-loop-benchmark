minor: The Batch decoder plan does not constrain or qualify allocation based on the untrusted `n` count before parsing inner frames. A tiny, valid-CRC Batch payload can declare `n = 0xffffffff`; preallocating `Records` to that count could cause a huge allocation or panic even though the bounded payload cannot contain that many frames. Reject counts above the maximum possible number of minimum-size frames in the remaining payload (each frame needs at least 9 bytes), or grow the result only as frames are successfully validated. This is distinct from the plan's explicit frame-length allocation guard.
No blocker or major issue remains.
