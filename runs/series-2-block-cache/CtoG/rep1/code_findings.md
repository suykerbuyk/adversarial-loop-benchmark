nit: callLoader recovers every panic from the Loader, including a runtime.Error, and returns it as a formatted error that embeds debug.Stack() (blockcache.go:194-205). Keep the recover, but put the stack in an unexported field or a verbose wrapper and re-panic a runtime.Error only if it can be shown not to have come from the Loader frame.
